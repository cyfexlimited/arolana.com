Privacy Policy

How Arolana protects your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Arolana collects, uses, protects, and manages your personal information when you use our marketplace.

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, place an order, contact support, register as a vendor, submit product information, or use any feature on Arolana.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, billing details, order information, vendor business details, support messages, and account preferences.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit Arolana, we may automatically collect certain information such as your device type, browser, IP address, pages visited, referral source, session information, and general usage activity.

This helps us improve security, understand customer behavior, prevent fraud, improve performance, and provide a better shopping experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process orders, payments, deliveries, returns, and refunds.
  • To connect customers with vendors and service providers.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To improve our marketplace, products, services, and user experience.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, suspicious activity, and security risks.
  • To send important updates about orders, accounts, payments, policies, and platform changes.
  • To send promotional messages where permitted, with an option to opt out.

4. Vendor and Marketplace Information

If you register as a vendor, we may collect business-related information such as store name, contact details, business address, product listings, verification details, payout details, and customer communication history.

Some vendor information may be displayed publicly on Arolana to help customers identify and communicate with sellers.

5. Payments and Transactions

Payments made on Arolana may be processed through trusted payment service providers. We do not store full card details on our servers unless clearly stated and securely handled by approved payment partners.

Transaction information may be used to confirm payment, prevent fraud, process refunds, settle vendors, and maintain accurate order records.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share necessary information with trusted parties who help us operate the marketplace.

  • Vendors, where needed to fulfil your order.
  • Delivery and logistics partners.
  • Payment processors and financial service providers.
  • Customer support, technical, and security service providers.
  • Regulators, law enforcement, or legal authorities where required by law.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Arolana may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, improve website performance, measure traffic, and personalize your experience.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, but disabling some cookies may affect how certain features work.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online platform can guarantee 100% security, so users are encouraged to keep their login details safe and report suspicious activity immediately.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable laws, you may request access to your personal information, ask for corrections, request deletion, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain communications.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details below.

10. Marketing Communications

We may send you promotional emails, offers, marketplace updates, or vendor-related opportunities. You may unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time.

Important service messages such as order updates, account security alerts, and payment notifications may still be sent when necessary.

11. Children’s Privacy

Arolana is not intended for use by children without the supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or marketplace operations.

When we update this policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Arolana.
