Return Policy

Simple, fair, and transparent returns.

Arolana offers a 7-day return policy on eligible items from the date of delivery. Products must be unused, in their original condition, and returned with all original packaging and accessories.

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

7-Day Return Window

Return requests must be made within 7 days from the date your item was delivered. The item must be unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging.

1. Items Eligible for Return

To qualify for a return, the product must meet the following conditions:

  • The return request must be made within 7 days of delivery.
  • The item must be unused and in its original condition.
  • The item must be returned with its original packaging.
  • All accessories, manuals, warranty cards, tags, and included items must be returned.
  • The item must not be damaged due to misuse, alteration, or improper handling.

2. Defective, Damaged, or Wrong Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, incomplete, or incorrect item, please contact Arolana Support within 7 days of delivery.

To help us resolve your request faster, include your order number, vendor name, clear photos or videos of the item, packaging, and any visible damage or error.

3. Items That May Not Be Returnable

Certain items may not be eligible for return due to product condition, hygiene, customization, activation, or manufacturer restrictions.

  • Customized or specially ordered products.
  • Software, digital products, license keys, subscriptions, or activated products.
  • Items damaged after delivery due to misuse or poor handling.
  • Clearance, final sale, or products marked as non-returnable.
  • Products missing accessories, packaging, serial numbers, manuals, or warranty cards.

4. How to Request a Return

  1. Contact Arolana Support within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return.
  3. Attach clear photos or videos where necessary.
  4. Wait for return approval and instructions.
  5. Send the item back using the approved return method.
  6. Wait for inspection and refund approval.

5. Return Shipping

Return shipping responsibility may depend on the reason for the return. If the return is caused by a wrong, damaged, incomplete, or defective item, Arolana or the vendor may assist according to the approved return process.

If the return is due to customer preference, wrong selection, or change of mind, return shipping costs may apply.

6. Inspection and Approval

Returned items will be inspected after they are received. If the item meets the return conditions, the refund or replacement process will continue.

If the item is used, damaged, incomplete, altered, or not in its original condition, the return may be rejected.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method or another approved refund channel where necessary.

Refund timelines may depend on payment provider processing time, bank processing time, and the completion of return inspection.

8. Vendor Marketplace Returns

Arolana is a multi-vendor marketplace. Some products are sold by independent vendors. Vendor return conditions may apply, but all vendors are expected to follow Arolana’s marketplace return standards.

Arolana may help review return issues between customers and vendors to promote fairness, trust, and buyer protection.

9. Need Help?

For return requests, damaged products, wrong deliveries, refund questions, or vendor-related issues, contact Arolana Support.
