About Arolana

Africa’s trusted marketplace for products, vendors, and services.

Arolana is a modern multi-vendor marketplace built to connect customers with verified sellers, reliable products, service providers, and secure shopping experiences across Africa.

Trusted Vendors

We help customers discover vendors with better visibility, transparency, and marketplace standards.

Local Logistics

Arolana is designed for real African commerce, including delivery, vendor coordination, and customer support.

Buyer Confidence

We are building a marketplace where customers can shop with more confidence and vendors can grow sustainably.

Our Mission

To make online buying and selling safer, easier, and more rewarding.

Our mission is to provide a seamless, secure, and enjoyable shopping experience while empowering vendors, installers, engineers, brands, and service providers to reach more customers.

  • Verified vendors and trusted marketplace standards
  • Quality products across multiple categories
  • Support for installation, repair, setup, and service needs
  • Secure payments, customer care, and vendor growth tools

What Makes Us Different

Arolana is not just a technology platform.

Arolana is a commerce ecosystem built for real products, real vendors, real customers, and real service needs. From everyday merchandise to professional equipment, our goal is to make buying and selling more trusted, organized, and scalable.
