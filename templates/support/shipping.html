Arolana Delivery Engine

Shipping fees are calculated by your location, route, speed, and package.

Arolana checkout now keeps delivery simple: choose Standard Delivery for reliable dispatch or Express Delivery for faster handling where available. Our system calculates the fee from pickup to drop-off distance, package weight, live rider demand, and service level.

Express Delivery

What customers receive

Delivery fee before payment

Checkout estimates delivery cost from your selected method, address, city, state, country, map distance, package weight, and current rider demand.

Assignment notification

You are notified when Arolana assigns the best available rider or approved delivery partner for your Standard or Express delivery.

Pickup notification

You receive a pickup update when the item leaves the vendor or dispatch point.

Live location updates

When admin, rider, or provider updates live location, Arolana sends a tracking notification and updates your order page.

Delivered and thank-you notifications

After delivery, you receive confirmation plus thank-you messages from Arolana and the vendor where available.

Delivery cost rule

Final delivery cost depends on location, package size, delivery method, pickup-to-drop-off distance, rider availability, and whether the order needs special handling.

Important If the final dispatch cost changes because of route or package constraints, Arolana support will confirm before dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter your order number or tracking code plus the order email if you are not signed in.

Who handles delivery?

Checkout uses Standard and Express delivery only. Arolana picks the best available delivery partner behind the scenes for your route.

What is the difference between Standard and Express?

Standard is the reliable default route. Express prioritizes faster dispatch where riders and delivery partners are available.

Do notifications work on phone?

Yes. Sign in, enable web notifications, and on iPhone open Arolana from the Home Screen app to receive web push notifications.
