Shipping & Delivery

Clear delivery fees, reliable dispatch, and better order tracking.

Arolana helps customers receive their orders through Standard or Express Delivery where available. Delivery fees may depend on location, route, package size, weight, service level, and delivery partner availability.

Standard Delivery

Standard Delivery is suitable for regular orders and reliable dispatch. The delivery fee is calculated based on your address, pickup route, package details, and available delivery options.

Express Delivery

Express Delivery gives faster handling where available. It may cost more depending on delivery partner availability, route demand, distance, package size, and urgency.

What Customers Receive

Arolana keeps customers informed from checkout to delivery, so every order can be tracked with better visibility and support.

Delivery fee before payment

Delivery cost is shown before checkout is completed, based on your selected delivery method, location, route, package weight, and available delivery support.

Delivery assignment update

You may receive an update when Arolana assigns an available rider, logistics provider, or approved delivery partner for your order.

Pickup notification

You receive an update when your item has been picked up from the vendor, warehouse, or dispatch point.

Tracking and status updates

Order status may be updated as your package moves through processing, pickup, transit, delivery attempt, and final delivery.

Delivery confirmation

After successful delivery, your order is marked as delivered and you may receive confirmation or a thank-you message from Arolana.

How Delivery Cost Is Calculated

Final delivery cost may depend on distance, location, package size, package weight, delivery method, route difficulty, rider availability, and special handling needs.

Important If dispatch cost changes because of route, package, or handling constraints, Arolana support may confirm with you before dispatch.

Delivery Protection

For delivery complaints, damaged packages, missing items, or wrong delivery updates, contact support with your order number, vendor name, tracking code, and photos where possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter your order number or tracking code. If you are signed in, you may also check from your account.

Who handles delivery?

Arolana may assign an available rider, logistics provider, vendor dispatch team, or approved delivery partner depending on your route and order type.

What is the difference between Standard and Express?

Standard is the regular delivery option. Express gives faster handling where available and may cost more depending on location and rider availability.

Will I receive delivery notifications?

Yes, where enabled. You may receive updates for order confirmation, pickup, transit, delivery attempt, and completed delivery.

Need help with a delivery?

Contact Arolana Support with your order number, delivery location, and tracking details.
