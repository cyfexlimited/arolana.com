Standard Delivery

Reliable delivery for regular orders. Fee is calculated from your address and provider route.

Express Delivery

Faster dispatch where available. Express may cost more depending on rider/provider availability.

Arolana Dispatch

Arolana riders or approved local riders assigned by admin for flexible local delivery.

Provider Delivery

Approved Arolana, vendor, independent, and logistics partners can be used based on route availability.

Pickup from Vendor

Collect directly from the vendor after Arolana and the vendor confirm pickup readiness.

What customers receive

Delivery fee before payment

Checkout estimates delivery cost from your selected method, provider, address, city, state, country, and current provider rules.

Assignment notification

You are notified when admin assigns a rider, driver, vendor pickup, or logistics provider.

Pickup notification

You receive a pickup update when the item leaves the vendor or dispatch point.

Live location updates

When admin, rider, or provider updates live location, Arolana sends a tracking notification and updates your order page.

Delivered and thank-you notifications

After delivery, you receive confirmation plus thank-you messages from Arolana and the vendor where available.

Delivery cost rule

Final delivery cost depends on location, package size, delivery method, provider route, rider availability, and whether the order needs special handling.

Important If a provider changes the final cost after checkout because of route or package constraints, Arolana support will confirm before dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter your order number or tracking code plus the order email if you are not signed in.

Can I choose a provider?

Yes. Checkout lets you choose a preferred provider, or you can let Arolana pick the best available provider for your route.

When is pickup from vendor available?

Pickup is available only after vendor confirmation. Arolana will notify you when pickup is ready.

Do notifications work on phone?

Yes. Sign in, enable web notifications, and on iPhone open Arolana from the Home Screen app to receive web push notifications.
